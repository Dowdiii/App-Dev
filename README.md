# 🎬 500 Days of Summer (2009)

---

## 💔 Not a Love Story

**Tagline:** "This is a story of boy meets girl. But you should know upfront, this is **not a love story**."

*500 Days of Summer* is a clever, non-linear romantic comedy-drama that explores a relationship from the perspective of **Tom Hansen** (Joseph Gordon-Levitt), who falls in love with **Summer Finn** (Zooey Deschanel), a woman who doesn't believe in true love.

> The film famously breaks the fourth wall and uses a 'Day Counter' to jump around the timeline of their 500 days together, highlighting the difference between expectation and reality.
